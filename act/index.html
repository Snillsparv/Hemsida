AI · From worry to action

What can I do?

Worried about where AI development is heading? You are not alone. Together we can influence it and steer towards a brighter future. Below are some first steps to take, wherever in the world you live.

The quickest one takes thirty seconds: signing the statement on superintelligence.

Sign the statement

Step 01 · thirty seconds

Sign the statement

The Statement on Superintelligence calls for a halt to the development of AI smarter than humans, until scientists agree it can be done safely and the public has had its say. Its signatories include Nobel laureates and AI pioneers such as Geoffrey Hinton and Yoshua Bengio. Every new signature adds pressure:

Sign →

Step 02 · five minutes at the keyboard

Write to your elected representatives

Politicians read their email, and the number of messages on an issue shapes how important it feels to them. Here is a ready-made email to start from. Rewriting it in your own words carries more weight:

Hello, I am one of your constituents, and I am increasingly worried about how fast AI development is moving. The leaders of the major AI companies now warn that we risk losing control, and Nobel laureates such as Geoffrey Hinton are calling for a halt to the development of superintelligence. So I want to ask: what is your plan if development continues at this pace? How are you preparing for rapid changes in the labour market, and do you support international agreements that regulate the most powerful AI systems? Your answer will decide how I vote next time. Kind regards [Your name]

Elsewhere: search for "contact my representative" together with your country. A short, personal email to one person beats a long one sent to everyone.

Step 03

Tell three people

Politics moves when enough people talk about an issue. Tell three people you know what is happening and why it matters. Share the video. Every person who understands the situation makes the question a little harder to ignore.

Step 04 · you do not have to do it alone

Join the movement

The largest organisation working to slow down AI development is PauseAI, with local groups in many countries. Join, find the group nearest you, and read more on their website:

Step 05 · for those who want more

Learn more

If you are young or thinking about changing careers: the AI safety field needs more people. 80,000 Hours guides you to careers that make a difference, and BlueDot runs free introductory courses on AI safety. To follow developments in depth, Zvi Mowshowitz's blog Don't Worry About the Vase is the best running summary of everything happening in AI (though very in-depth).
